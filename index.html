Websites for creatives who care deeply how their work is perceived.

I design and build small, intentional websites for artists, studios, and practitioners. Custom, quick, and made by a creative for creatives who want their work to feel considered—not templated.

Who is this for?

Artists, designers, yoga teachers, therapists, musicians, and small studios who need a clear, beautiful online presence that reflects the quality of their work.

Want a site that truly showcases your work?
Let's build one together.

What do you get?

  • Custom, intentional website:
    A one-page or small multi-page site designed specifically for your work and your style.
  • Seamless experience for all visitors
    Your website will look beautiful and be easy to navigate on phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Simple setup and launch
    I handle hosting, domain connection, and the technical details so you don’t have to worry.
  • Clear, organized presentation
    Your work, services, or offerings are displayed in a way that’s easy for visitors to understand and act on.
  • Fast turnaround
    From start to launch in 7-10 days, with one revision included to ensure it’s exactly right.
  • Friendly guidance
    I’ll help you present your work clearly without extra stress, so your site feels polished and intentional from day one.

Process

  1. Quick Intake: We’ll chat about your work, goals, and what you want your website to do.
  2. Design & Build: I create a custom, polished site tailored to your style and audience.
  3. Revision: One round of edits to make sure everything is just right.
  4. Launch: Your site goes live — I handle domain connection and hosting so you can start sharing immediately.

About Claire Cody

I’m a creative technologist & digital designer with a background in front-end development, graphic design, creative digital interactivity, and analog art. I focus on visual clarity, structure, and stable systems that ensure your work is presented in a way that feels professional, accessible, and true to your style.

I work under StudioCeiz, my creative studio, but at this stage it’s just me — personally handling every project to make sure your website reflects the care and thought you put into your work.
